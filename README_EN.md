### 🚀 About Me
I am a **Mid-level Software Developer** (full stack) with a strong foundation in **UX/UI design**. I specialize in the full lifecycle—from high-fidelity prototypes to high-performance implementations across web and mobile.

- 💼 **Recent experience:** As a mid-level full stack developer at **DevMagic**, I worked on the large-scale SaaS ecosystem **Bloom by IMPACT** (corporate ESG management), delivering high-performance modules such as dashboards with **25,000+ virtualized records**, a secure Node.js/PostgreSQL back end, and leading mobile maintenance with **React Native**.
- 🎓 **Education:** Bachelor’s in **Information Systems (UNIP)**, UX Design specialization (XP Educação/IGTI), higher education in web development, and a solid background in network infrastructure / TCP-IP (SENAI).
- 🧠 **What sets me apart:** Combining **corporate design systems and clean code**. I have a strong track record building highly faithful interfaces focused on performance, accessibility (WCAG), and scalable architectures. I also actively use **generative AI** to stay highly productive as a mid-level developer.
